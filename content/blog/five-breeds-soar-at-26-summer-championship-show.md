Milk Source Genetics continued its impressive show streak at the Wisconsin Summer Championship Show with two Grand Champions and an assortment of other awards:

**Ayrshires**

Bricker-Farms King **Cinderella** *(pictured)* ** ** won Grand Champion and 1st 4 Yr. Old (Co-owned w/ K & M Wolf and J & S Fairbanks)

&nbsp;

**Holsteins**

Jacobs Chief **Dina** won Grand Champion and 1st 4 Yr. Old. (Co-owned w/ Ransom Rail Farms)

Ms Be **Tempted**\-Red-ET won 1st Milking Yearling.

Budjon-Vail Eyec **Chalack**\-ET garnered 1st Junior 3 Yr. Old.

Oak-Ridge-K Gchip **Turbo** was the 1st Place and Best Udder in the Production Cow Class

Harvestacre Champagne **Showers** was 2nd Fall Senior 2 Yr. Old.

Maifield Lambda **Monique** was 3rd Place and 2nd Place Junior in the Winter 2 Yr. Old Class

&nbsp;

**Red & Whites**

Ms Be **Tempted**\-Red-ET won Honorable Mention (HM) Intermediate Champion, 1st Milking Yearling along with Best Udder (BU) and Best Bred & Owned (BO). (Co-owned with Crescentmead, Stein-Crest Holsteins , and Laurie Fischer)

Durkacres Apple **Tonic**\-Red captured HM Senior Champ as well as 2nd Place 4 Yr. Old.

Dridge Dc Hot **Tamale**\-Red-ET won Junior Champion and 1st Summer Yearling.

Congratulations also to MS Genetics-bred Milksource **Tantalyze**\-Red-ET, exhibited by Taylor Haeft, who won 2nd 5 Yr. Old.

&nbsp;

**Brown Swiss**

Birdsallway Time Out **Nita**\-ET, exhibited by Tristen Ostrom, won Jr. Champ of the Junior and Open Shows as well as 1st Fall Yearling. (Co-owned with the Kinnard family)

Seths Collaps **Princess** ETV won 1st Spring Junior 2 Yr. Old. (BU)

Harvest Brz Els **Windstorm**, exhibited by Tristen Ostrom, garnered Grand Champion of the Junior Show, Intermediate Champ of the Jr. & Open Shows as well as 1st Junior 3 Yr. Old (BU)

MS Trickster **Doubledoink** placed 2nd in the Winter Calf.

Jenlar Diego **Wedding**, exhibited by Tristen Ostrom, earned 2nd Sr. 2 Yr. Old and 1st Junior 2 Yr. Old.

&nbsp;

**Jerseys**

GMBV Joel **Dixie**\-ET won Res. Grand Champion, Res. Senior Champ and 1st 5 Yr. Old (BU).

Milksource **Harmony** captured Res. Grand Champ, Intermediate Champion and Champ Bred & Owned of the Junior Show. She was also Res. Intermediate Champion of the Open Show as well as 2nd Junior 3 Yr. Old.

Koss-Reuter Dream of **Heidi**\-ET won Reserve Junior Champion and 1st Winter Calf.

Milksource **Miss America** was 3rd Spring Junior 2 Yr. Old.

Milksource **All About It**\-ET was 4th Place 4 Yr. Old.
