In spectacular fashion, MilkSource Genetics' Holstein **PWRUP** won the Reserve Supreme Champion title at the 2026 World Dairy Expo.

**PWRUP** along with the Ayrshire **Cinderella** were also named the top cows in their respective breeds.

This year's highlights include:

&nbsp;

**Holsteins**

Eixdale **PWRUP** Alongside also won Grand Champion, Senior Champion, 1st 5 Yr. Old and Best Udder.

Jacobs Chief **Dina** captured Honorable Mention Sr. Champ, 1st 4 Yr. Old and Best Udder.

Hill View Randall **Praline** won 2nd Fall Sr. 2 Yr. Old.

Milksource **Pony Up**\-ET was 4th Fall Calf.

&nbsp;

**Ayrshires**

Bricker-Farms King **Cinderella** captured Grand Champion, Senior Champ and 1st 4 Yr. Old.

&nbsp;

**Jerseys**

GMBV Joel **Dixie**\-ET won Reserve Grand Champion, Res. Senior Champion and 1st 5 Yr. Old.

Highview Balin **Journey** earned 3rd Production Cow.

&nbsp;

**Red & Whites**

Dridge DC **Hot Tamale**\-Red ET won Junior Champion of the Open Show and 1st Summer Yearling.

Ms Be **Tempted**\-Red-ET was 2nd Milking Yearling and best Bred & Owned.

Ms Ransom-Rail **Beth**\-Red captured 2nd Production Cow.

Golden-Oaks **Temptres**\-Red-ET earned 3rd Aged Cow.

&nbsp;

**Brown Swiss**

Harvest Brz Els **Windstorm** won Intermediate Champion of the Junior Show and 1st Jr. 3 Yr. Old (1st Junior and Best Udder).

Jenlar Diego **Wedding** ETV captured Res. Intermediate Champ of the Junior Show and 4th Sr. 2 Yr. Old (1st Junior).

Birdsallway Time Out **Nita**\-ET was 1st Place Fall Yearling.

Seths Collaps **Princess** ETV was 4th Spring Junior 2 Yr. Old.

&nbsp;

**Junior Holstein Show**

Milksource **Pony Up**\-ET won 1st Fall Calf and Maifield Lambda **Monique** earned 1st Winter Sr. 2 Yr. Old.
